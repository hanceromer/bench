Support

Write to us with any question, problem or idea about Bench. We usually reply within a few days.

benchhapp@gmail.com

You can also write from inside the app: the Contact us link at the bottom of the Profile tab opens an email that includes the app version.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Account settings → Delete my account. Your profile, reviews, photos and likes are permanently deleted. If you cannot access the app, write to us from the email address registered on your account.

I saw inappropriate content or a user who bothers me

Use the report button on the bench page or next to the review. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours and content that breaks the rules is removed. You can block a user from the report menu next to their review or from their profile page; you will no longer see their reviews or photos.

How do I sign in?

You can browse the map and benches without signing in. To add benches, rate, like and report, use Sign in with Apple or a one-time code sent to your email.

What is my location used for?

Only while the app is open, to show benches near you and to place a bench you add. Your location is not stored on our servers. You can turn the permission off at any time in your phone's settings.
