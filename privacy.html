Privacy Policy

Bench is an app where people photograph, rate and share public benches on a map. This policy explains which personal data is processed when you use Bench, why, and what rights you have, including under Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

1. Data controller

The data controller for your personal data is Ömer Faruk Hançer. For anything about this policy or your data, write to benchhapp@gmail.com.

2. What data we process

  • Account details: your email address (if you sign in with email) or the account identifier and email address Apple passes to us (if you sign in with Apple; with "Hide My Email" we only see Apple's relay address), your username and, if you add one, your profile photo.
  • Guest use: When you browse without signing in, you are assigned a randomly generated anonymous identifier. It is not linked to your name or email and is only used to remember the benches you saved.
  • Content you share: the name, description, location and photos of benches you add; your ratings, reviews, selected features and best-time information; your likes on reviews.
  • Data only you can see: benches you saved, users you blocked and reports you made.
  • Location: If you allow it, your location is used on your device to show benches near you and to place benches you add. When you rate a bench, your current location is sent to the server to verify that you are at the bench; it is deleted immediately after the distance is calculated and is not stored. We keep no location history and never track your location in the background.
  • Photos: Photos are re-encoded on your device before upload; hidden information such as where the photo was taken and the device model (EXIF) is removed.
  • Notifications: If you allow notifications, your device’s notification address (push token) and your app language are stored with your account so we can send notifications to your device. You can turn this off at any time in your phone’s settings; the record is deleted when you sign out or delete your account.
  • Technical logs: Our infrastructure provider keeps connection logs such as IP address and request time for a limited period for security and abuse prevention.
  • Waitlist (website): your email address, the city you optionally choose, the link you came from and the time you gave consent.

Bench has no ads and no ad tracking; we use no third-party analytics or tracking tools. Your language and theme preferences and your session are stored only on your device.

3. Why we process it and on what legal basis

  • To create your account, sign you in and show the content you share in the app: performance of our contract with you (KVKK Art. 5/2-c).
  • To prevent fake ratings, unwanted content and abuse, to review reports and to keep the service safe: our legitimate interest (KVKK Art. 5/2-f).
  • To meet our legal obligations and respond to lawful requests from authorities: legal obligation (KVKK Art. 5/2-ç).
  • To send you Bench announcements if you joined the waitlist: your explicit consent (KVKK Art. 5/1). You can withdraw it at any time.

Location, camera and photos are only accessed if you grant permission on your device; you can turn these permissions off at any time in your phone's settings.

4. Information everyone can see

Your username, profile photo, badges, the benches you add with their location and photos, your ratings and your reviews are visible to everyone who uses Bench. Your email address is never shown to other users.

Please make sure identifiable people do not appear in your photos; content containing other people's personal data is removed.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data or share it with anyone for marketing. To run the service we only work with these providers:

  • Supabase: database, authentication and photo storage. Your data is stored on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Resend: your email address is passed to this service to email you your sign-in code (USA).
  • Expo and Apple: Notifications are delivered through Expo’s push service and Apple’s push notification service; your device’s notification address and the notification text are passed to these services (USA).
  • Apple: if you sign in with Apple, authentication goes through Apple; on iPhone the map is shown with Apple Maps.
  • OpenStreetMap: Some benches on the map come from OpenStreetMap open data. Only the map area being viewed is queried; your identity is not sent.

Because these providers' servers are outside Turkey, your data is transferred abroad (KVKK Art. 9). Otherwise your data is only shared with authorities when the law requires it.

6. How long we keep data

Your account details and content are kept for as long as your account is open. When you delete your account, your profile, reviews, photos and likes are deleted. Benches that others have also rated stay on the map without your name.

Removal from infrastructure backups and connection logs may take a short while longer after deletion. Your waitlist email is kept until you ask to leave the list or the announcements end.

7. Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your account in the app: Profile → Account settings → Delete my account. If you cannot access the app, write to benchhapp@gmail.com from the email address registered on your account.

You can also edit or delete individual items (benches, reviews, photos) in the app.

8. Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, to request information about it, to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly, to know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom it is transferred, to have it corrected if incomplete or inaccurate, to have it erased or destroyed, to have these actions notified to third parties to whom it was transferred, to object to an adverse outcome produced by automated analysis, and to claim compensation if you suffer damage from unlawful processing.

Send your request to benchhapp@gmail.com. We reply free of charge within 30 days at the latest. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board.

If you live in the European Union or another country with a similar data protection law, you can also use the rights that law gives you, such as access, rectification, erasure, portability and objection, through the same address.

9. Children

Bench is not designed for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If we notice such an account we delete it; if you notice one, please write to us.

10. Security

Your data is sent over an encrypted connection. In the database, each user can only access their own private data and only change their own content. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security; in case of a breach we make the notifications the law requires.

11. Changes to this policy

When we update this policy we publish the new version here and change the date at the top. We announce important changes in the app.
